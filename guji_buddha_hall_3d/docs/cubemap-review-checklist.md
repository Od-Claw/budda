# Cubemap Candidate Review Checklist

Only promote a generated candidate after checking all items.

1. `nz` is the front Buddha view.
2. `px` and `nx` are the right and left shrine walls with small Buddha niches.
3. `pz` is the rear view and does not contain another central giant Buddha.
4. `py` is the tree canopy / sky / upper ruin.
5. `ny` is the stone floor / leaves / courtyard ground.
6. The six faces share one light direction and color temperature.
7. Stone wall material is consistent across faces.
8. Buddha statue style is consistent across faces.
9. There is no text, watermark, UI, modern object, or person.
10. Face boundaries are not severely broken when viewed in Three.js.
11. The Three.js view is visibly clearer than the current stable cubemap.
12. Lamp hotspots still roughly align with shrine niches.
13. Offerings still appear near the altar table.

If any item fails, keep the run as an experiment and do not promote it to `public/assets/cubemap/4k`.
